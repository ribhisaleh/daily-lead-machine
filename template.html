How this board is built

  • Signal: public posts on X where a business is hiring for — or asking for an agency for — AI automation, voice AI, chatbots, or Gulf Arabic voice AI. The open role is the personalization hook.
  • Scoring is automated and heuristic. Flags (off-geo, supplement-health, too-big, agency-competitor, role-not-sold, spam) come from your business's ICP and auto-reject rules. "Review" rows are borderline and need a human read before outreach. Nothing here has been contacted.
  • Known gap: OnlineJobs.ph posts are readable but employer-anonymized (no company, country, or contact on the public page), so they can't be scored to a contactable lead and are excluded. Facebook / Instagram / Threads job posts are login-gated and not yet wired in.
